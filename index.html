About Us

WildCircuits is a FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) robotics team passionate about engineering, programming, innovation, and teamwork. Our members work together to design, build, program, and test a competitive robot while developing valuable STEM, leadership, and problem-solving skills. We strive to inspire creativity, collaborate with our community, and continuously learn through every challenge we face.

🌟 Our Rookie Season

The 2026–2027 FIRST Tech Challenge season marks the beginning of WildCircuits' journey as an FTC team. This is our very first year competing in FIRST Tech Challenge, and we are excited to learn, grow, and challenge ourselves throughout the season. As a rookie team, every design, line of code, and competition experience helps us build a strong foundation for the future. We look forward to developing our engineering skills, working together, and representing our team with pride.
