ROOKIE SEASON / REAL WORK

Built in Robbinsville.
Ready to learn.

WildCircuits is a FIRST Tech Challenge team from Robbinsville, New Jersey. The 2026–2027 BIOBUZZ season is our first FTC season.

We build, program, design in CAD, and connect through outreach. Building the Future — One Robot at a Time isn’t just a tagline. It’s the work we’re here to do.
